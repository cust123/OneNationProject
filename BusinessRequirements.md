# Business Requirements Document: OneNationRide App

## 1. Project Overview

### Vision Statement

To create Pakistan's leading first community driven ride-sharing platform that connects travelers with vehicle owners, making intercity travel more accessible, affordable..

### Business Objectives

1. Provide an easy and affordable means of travels for professionals
2. Create income opportunities for vehicle owners
3. Establish a trusted community-driven travel network
4. Achieve 100,000 active users within the first year
5. Maintain a user satisfaction rate above 4.5/5

## 2. Stakeholders

### Primary Stakeholders

- Travelers (Ride Requesters)
- Vehicle Owners
- Business Operations Team
- Customer Support Team
- Marketing Team
- Finance Team

### Secondary Stakeholders

- Payment Service Providers
- Insurance Partners
- Regulatory Bodies
- Local Transportation Authorities

## 6. Success Criteria

### 6.1 Launch Phase (First 6 Months)

- 4000 registered users
- Coverage in Islamabad, Peshawar, M major cities
- Average rating of 4.3/5
- 80% booking completion rate

### 6.2 Growth Phase (6-12 Months)

- 10,000 active users
- Coverage in 10 major cities
- Average rating of 4.5/5
- 90% booking completion rate
